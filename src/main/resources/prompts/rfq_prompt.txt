CRITICAL RULE #1 — PURCHASE QUANTITY
========================================================

"Quantity: 20 Units" → quantity = 20
"Quantity: seven units" → quantity = 7
"Quantity: ten pieces" → quantity = 10
"Quantity: two thousand units" → quantity = 2000

- "5-star rating" → RATING (quantity = null)

CRITICAL RULE #4 — NUMBER WORDS
========================================================

Examples:
"seven units" → 7

"one thousand units" → 1000

"capacity of ten thousand liters per hour" → THIS IS NOT QUANTITY! quantity = null.

========================================================
CRITICAL RULE #5 — CONTEXT-BASED QUANTITY EXTRACTION
========================================================
A number is a purchase quantity ONLY when it is associated with purchasing/order language such as:
Quantity, Qty, Required quantity, Required, Need, We need, Require, Required units, Units required, Pieces required, Nos required, Number of units, Number required, We require X units, Please quote X units.

Example:
"We require 5 Industrial Water Treatment Systems." → quantity = 5
"Please quote for 5 systems." → quantity = 5
"Quantity: 5 systems." → quantity = 5

But:
"Industrial Water Treatment System with 10,000 LPH capacity." → quantity = null
